The problem

It's not you.
It's the business model.

Privacy feels impossible because the internet was built to collect, and now AI is built to act. Here's the problem from four angles, why it matters to you, and what to do about it without losing sleep.

01 · Money
You're the product.

What's happening: Apps, ad networks, and data brokers collect where you go, what you buy, and who you know, then sell it to anyone who pays.

Why care: It's how scammers, stalkers, and bosses find you, and it's legal in most places.

Calm move: Opt out of people-search sites; turn off ad tracking.

02 · Machines
Scams got an engine.

What's happening: AI writes perfect phishing, clones voices from seconds of audio, and runs attacks with little human help. Even the labs' own agents have gone rogue.

Why care: Being a small target no longer protects you. Attacks are cheap enough to try on everyone.

Calm move: Security keys on email; a family code word.

03 · Power
Surveillance isn't equal.

What's happening: Organizers, workers, immigrants, survivors, and journalists get watched most, by employers, agencies, abusers, and bad actors.

Why care: Privacy is how people with less power stay safe enough to speak, organize, and seek help.

Calm move: Separate your organizing identity from the rest of your life.

04 · People
Your gap is our gap.

What's happening: Convenience and attention-grabbing design push us into habits we didn't choose: one login for everything, giant group chats, AI with the keys.

Why care: One person's weak spot becomes a whole group's breach. Security is collective.

Calm move: Name an owner for every group chat and shared drive.
