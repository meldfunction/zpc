Zen Privacy Collective

Privacy as intentional practice, not paranoia. We build literacy, infrastructure, and community for people and organizations who want to protect what matters.

Join our workshops Learn more

For whom

Individuals who care about digital autonomy. Organizations handling sensitive data or communications. Communities building resilience in surveillance-heavy landscapes. Anyone who wants to understand and protect their digital life without feeling overwhelmed.

What we do

Hands-on workshops

Learn digital hygiene, secure communications, local AI infrastructure, computer use security, and how to build organizational privacy systems that stick.

Organizational accompaniment

Work with organizations to design privacy practices, governance structures, and cultures that protect sensitive work and build member trust.

Open tools and research

Contribute to open-source projects, publish research on privacy infrastructure, and share methodologies for threat modeling and policy development.

Our approach

We're grounded in digital rights principles (EFF), privacy-first encrypted tools (Proton), rigorous research methodology (OSINT), and hands-on security community practice (DEFCON culture). We build literacy alongside infrastructure. We know that privacy is a discipline, not a destination, and that good practice is better than perfect tools.
