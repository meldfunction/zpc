Education & Workshops

Hands-on workshops that turn privacy knowledge into practice. Each session includes prerequisite materials, live practice, and take-home tools.

Workshop series

Our fall 2026 series runs Fridays 6:30-8 PM in Richmond, VA. Each workshop stands alone but builds on previous sessions. Participants receive a take-home playbook, discussion recordings, and community access to peer learning.

Workshop 1: Digital Hygiene Foundations

October 2, 2026
6:30-8 PM ET · In person + virtual option

Learn threat modeling for individuals and small organizations. We'll inventory your digital exposure, identify who wants what about you, and build a personal privacy baseline.

What you'll learn:

  • What constitutes personally identifiable information and why it matters
  • Threat modeling basics: adversaries, assets, and likelihood
  • Data inventory: where your data lives and who holds it
  • Creating a personal digital hygiene checklist

Prerequisites: Free 1-hour course: "Digital Privacy Fundamentals" (link provided on registration)

Workshop 2: Secure Communications & Tools

October 9, 2026
6:30-8 PM ET · In person + virtual option

Move beyond app choices to protocol design. We'll build a communications stack for your team, evaluate tools critically, and practice secure practices that stick.

What you'll learn:

  • End-to-end encryption: what it is and what it isn't
  • Evaluating communication tools critically (Proton, Signal, Matrix/Element, etc.)
  • Building team communication policies that work
  • Operational security (OPSEC) for remote teams
  • Incident response for compromised communications

Prerequisites: Workshop 1 or equivalent knowledge

Workshop 3: Local AI Infrastructure & Privacy Preservation

October 16, 2026
6:30-8 PM ET · In person + virtual option

Run AI tools without cloud vendors. We'll set up local LLMs, explore privacy-preserving compute patterns, and understand data sovereignty in practice.

What you'll learn:

  • How cloud AI models train on user data (and what that means)
  • Local-first AI infrastructure: Ollama, open models, and more
  • Privacy-preserving techniques: differential privacy, federated learning
  • Hands-on: Setting up Ollama with a current open-weight model locally
  • Self-hosted AI assistants for organizations: what to run, where, and who can see the data

Prerequisites: Basic command-line comfort or Workshop 1+2

Workshop 4: Computer Use Security & OPSEC

October 23, 2026
6:30-8 PM ET · In person + virtual option

Secure your machine and your habits. We'll walk through device hardening, browser isolation, and the human practices that matter more than tools.

What you'll learn:

  • Device inventory and threat assessment
  • Operating system hardening (macOS, Linux, Windows)
  • Browser isolation and privacy configurations
  • Password management and secret handling
  • Detecting compromise: what to look for (see our common security fails)
  • Operational security habits for high-risk work

Prerequisites: Workshops 1-3 or equivalent

Workshop 5: Organizational Privacy Systems (Capstone)

October 30, 2026
6:30-8 PM ET · In person + virtual option

Build privacy governance that sticks. We'll design policies, create accountability structures, and align privacy with your organization's values and constraints.

What you'll learn:

  • Privacy policies that teams actually follow
  • Governance structures for privacy decisions
  • Training and culture change
  • Audit and accountability mechanisms
  • Building a privacy-first culture over time

Prerequisites: Workshops 1-4 or equivalent knowledge

Format & community

Each 90-minute session combines presentation, hands-on practice, and small-group discussion. We limit in-person attendance to 20 people to keep learning intimate. Virtual participants join live and can ask questions in real time. Recordings are available to registered participants for 90 days.

All sessions are scheduled for accessibility. Childcare and ASL interpretation available on request. Sliding scale pricing available; no one turned away for lack of funds.

For organizations

We offer customized workshops tailored to your organization's privacy risks, tech stack, and constraints. Recent engagements include privacy bootcamps for nonprofits, secure communications trainings for legal teams, and organizational privacy governance design.
