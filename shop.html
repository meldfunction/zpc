How to buy safely

Buy direct. Get security keys, phones, and encrypted drives from the maker or an authorized reseller, not marketplace third-party sellers. Counterfeit and tampered security devices do show up on big marketplaces.
  • Check that packaging is sealed and tamper-evident. Return anything that looks opened.
  • Buy two security keys and register both, so a lost key isn't a lockout.
  • Ship to a PO box or pickup point if your home address is sensitive.
  • Ask vendors about nonprofit pricing; many offer it. Registered nonprofits can also check TechSoup.
  • Buy for your threat model, not your anxiety. The tags below help.
